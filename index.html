    Your accounts

    Import MT4 / MT5 history

    This reads a report file that you export yourself. It does not connect to your broker. In MT4, open the Account History tab, right-click and choose Save as Detailed Report. In MT5, open the History tab, right-click, choose Report, then HTML. Menu names differ between versions and brokers.

    MT4 / MT5 live sync

    This site currently imports exported history reports; it does not yet perform automatic broker sync. Investor passwords are read-only, but they are still account credentials. A GitHub Pages front end cannot safely connect to broker servers by itself. Live sync needs a separately hosted connector and your own account with that provider.

    MetaApi documents investor-password connections for read-only access. Review their credential handling, account costs, and terms before choosing a connector. Never enter a master trading password here.
